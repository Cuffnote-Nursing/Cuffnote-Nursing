# Cuffnote Privacy Policy

*Effective October 3, 2026 · version 2026-10-03*

> **Study aid only.** Cuffnote is a study aid only. It is not medical advice and must never be used to care for a real patient. Content can be wrong, simplified or out of date, so always verify it against credible, current sources: your instructors, textbooks, facility policies and official drug references.

## The short version

- You can use Cuffnote **without an account**. Your progress then stays on your own device and we never receive it.
- If you **create an account**, we store your email address, an optional first name and your study progress so you can restore it on any device.
- We do **not** sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
- You can **permanently delete your account and cloud data** yourself at any time inside the app.
- Never enter patient information anywhere in Cuffnote.

## 1. Who we are

Cuffnote is an independent study app ("we", "us"). We are not a school, employer, hospital or testing body. You can reach us at [cuffnoteapp@gmail.com](mailto:cuffnoteapp@gmail.com).

## 2. What we collect

**If you do not create an account**, the following is saved only on your device (in your browser or app storage) and is never sent to us: your progress (points, streak, flashcard schedule, quiz results, topics you marked as studied), cards and questions you created, your settings (theme, nursing-school stage), and, if you set up the AI tutor, your AI sign-in or API key.

**If you create an account**, we also store:

- your email address and, if you give one, your first name;
- your password, handled by Google Firebase Authentication (stored in hashed form by Google; we cannot read it);
- a copy of your study progress and nursing-school stage (the items listed above); your AI key and theme are never uploaded;
- a record that you accepted this policy and the Study Disclaimer (the version and the time);
- basic technical data that Google's services process to run and protect the service, such as IP address and device or browser type.

**AI tutor.** When you use the AI features, what you type (and any picture you choose to send) goes **straight from your device to the AI provider you selected** (OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI/ChatGPT, Anthropic/Claude, or another OpenAI-compatible service whose address you enter) using your own account or key. We do not receive, see or store it, and your key stays on your device. Those providers have their own terms and may charge your account; free models and free tiers may log requests or use them to improve their systems. Do not send personal or patient information.

**What we do not collect:** advertising identifiers, location, contacts or microphone data. Cuffnote uses your camera or photo library only if you choose to add a picture for the ECG AI feature, and that picture goes only to the AI provider you picked.

## 3. How we use it

To create and secure your account, store and sync your progress between your devices, send account emails (email verification and password reset), prevent abuse, and answer your requests. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it or share it for marketing.

## 4. Who handles your data

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores account and progress data for us as our service provider.
- **The web host** that serves the Cuffnote app files may keep standard server logs (for example IP addresses).
- **AI providers**, only when you choose to use the AI tutor, as described above.
- **Authorities**, only if the law requires us to share information.

Your cloud data is stored in Google data centres, in the region chosen when the service was set up (the United States unless stated otherwise on our website). By creating an account you agree to this transfer and storage.

## 5. How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. After deletion it is removed from our database straight away; Google may keep backups or logs for a limited period before they are overwritten. Data on your device stays until you sign out (signing out uploads your latest progress and then clears it from the device), clear the app's storage, or uninstall.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Delete:** in the app go to Settings, then Account, then Manage, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your login and your saved progress from our database. See "Deleting your account" below.
- **Access and copy:** your progress is visible in the app, and Settings, then Back up, saves a copy of the data on your device. To get a copy of your cloud data, contact us.
- **Correct or change:** you can change your progress and cards in the app; contact us to correct your email or name.
- **Stop:** you can stop using the account at any time by signing out or deleting it.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California) you may also have the right to access, port, correct or erase your data, to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your data protection authority. We offer the same controls to everyone. We do not sell personal information.

## 7. Children

Cuffnote is meant for nursing students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Patient and health information

Cuffnote is a study tool, not a medical record system, and is not designed to handle protected health information. Never enter patient names, record numbers, images or any information that could identify a person.

## 9. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Database rules allow each signed-in account to read and change only its own record. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase. No online service is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password, do not reuse it, and sign out on shared devices.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date and version above, and for important changes we will tell you in the app and may ask you to accept it again.

## 11. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about privacy: [cuffnoteapp@gmail.com](mailto:cuffnoteapp@gmail.com).

## Deleting your account

**In the app (fastest):**

1. Open Cuffnote and tap the **gear** icon (Settings).
2. Under **Account**, tap **Manage**, then scroll to **Delete account**.
3. Enter your password and tap **Delete my account**, then confirm.

This **permanently** deletes your login (email, name and password) and your saved progress from our database, and clears Cuffnote data from that device. It cannot be undone. Forgot your password? On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password?**, choose a new one from the email, sign in, then delete.

**If you cannot use the app,** email [cuffnoteapp@gmail.com](mailto:cuffnoteapp@gmail.com) from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Cuffnote account". We will delete the account within 30 days.

**What is deleted:** your account (email, name, login), your cloud progress record, and the consent record. **What may remain briefly:** backups or logs held by our service providers until they are overwritten. Data saved only on other devices you used stays there until you sign out, clear the app's storage or uninstall.
